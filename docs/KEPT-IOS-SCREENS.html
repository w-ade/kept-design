v0.0.0 iOS screens

Skeleton wireframes for the first native build, drawn to iPhone 17 Pro geometry: 402×874pt, 59pt top inset, 34pt bottom inset. Every measurement is in points. Derived from the web routes in src/kept/KeptApp.tsx. A starting point for p8, not a settled spec.

Body, screen and Dynamic Island are measured off the iPhone 17 Pro Figma frame: 402×874pt at r56, 8pt bezel, 112×32pt island 13pt down. The 59/34pt safe areas and the iOS 26 floating tab bar are still from memory. Check those two against Xcode before building to them.

Library /library

From KeptLibrary.tsx · 96pt large title · 83pt tab bar

  1. 1Large title nav bar: 96pt total (44pt bar + 52pt title area), 34pt bold title on a 16pt margin. Collapses to the 44pt inline bar on scroll — that transition is free on iOS and is most of why the screen feels native.
  2. 2Search is a UISearchController in the nav bar, 36pt tall, revealed by pull-down rather than always on screen. The web puts it inline; do not port that.
  3. 3Collection grid, KeptFigureGrid on web. 3 across inside 16pt margins with 2pt gutters gives 122pt cells at this width.
  4. 4New collection goes in the nav bar trailing slot as a +, opening a sheet. The web dialog does not translate.
  5. 5Tab bar is 49pt above a 34pt home indicator inset, 83pt total. On iOS 26 this is the floating Liquid Glass bar that contracts on scroll, not a flush bar — worth confirming against the current HIG before building.

Collection /library/:collectionId

From KeptCollection.tsx · 44pt inline nav · 83pt tab bar

  1. 1Inline 44pt nav bar with a back chevron and the parent title. Web draws KeptCrumbs breadcrumbs; iOS gets the native back plus the edge-swipe, so the breadcrumb row is dropped.
  2. 2Description sits under the title. It is editable in place on web — on iOS that wants an explicit Edit affordance rather than a focusable paragraph.
  3. 3The grid uses the thumb/ 480px copies, which is what oklab-squares just generated.
  4. 4Tapping a cell should be a zoom transition from the tapped cell, not a push. That is the shot at making it feel better than the web.

Reference /library/:collectionId/:referenceId

From KeptReference.tsx · 44pt inline nav · 83pt tab bar

  1. 1The full/ 1200px image, edge to edge at 402pt wide rather than inside the 16pt margin. Pinch to zoom.
  2. 2Pins are a coordinate overlay on the image (KeptPin), not a control in a layout. This is the single hardest thing on the list to port and it does not get easier by waiting.
  3. 3Horizontal swipe steps between references. Web uses the keyboard and a KeptPager — the pager row disappears on iOS.
  4. 4Caption and notes below the fold: “What is worth remembering about this one?”
  5. 5Tags as 44pt-tap-target chips.
  6. 6483 lines on web, the largest screen. Most likely candidate for splitting — image and metadata may want to be a sheet over the image rather than one scroll.
